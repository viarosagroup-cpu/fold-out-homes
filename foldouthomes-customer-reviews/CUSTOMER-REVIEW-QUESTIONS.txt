FOLD OUT HOMES CUSTOMER REVIEW QUESTIONS

Purchase verification (kept private)
1. What is your full name?
2. What email address did you use for your purchase?
3. What is your order or invoice number? (Optional)
4. Where are you up to: order placed, delivered, installed, under 3 months, 3 to 12 months, or over 12 months?
5. Which Fold Out Homes model or style did you purchase?
6. What size is your home: 20 ft, 30 ft, 40 ft, or custom?

Ratings (1 = very poor, 5 = excellent)
7. How would you rate the product quality, including the build, materials and finishes?
8. How accurately did the completed home match what was shown and agreed?
9. How would you rate our communication, helpfulness and response times?
10. How would you rate delivery, setup and handover?
11. How would you rate the overall value for money?
12. From 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend Fold Out Homes?

Written feedback
13. What did we do well?
14. What could we improve?
15. What name and location would you like displayed with your review? For example, "Paul R., Brisbane".
16. Do you give Fold Out Homes permission to publish your ratings and comments after customer verification?
17. May we contact you about supplying customer photos or a short video testimonial?

SCORING METHOD
The customer trust score is shown out of 10 and uses these weights:
- Product quality: 30%
- Accuracy: 15%
- Communication: 15%
- Delivery and setup: 25%
- Value for money: 15%

Only verified customer reviews should be included in the public combined score.
